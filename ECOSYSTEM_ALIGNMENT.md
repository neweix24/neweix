# Ecosystem alignment

**Canonical authority:** [Supreme Ecosystem Governance Charter](https://github.com/neweix24/supreme/blob/main/docs/ecosystem-governance-charter.md)

- Supreme is the highest and final authority for policy, reserved approvals, Admin delegation, suspension/revocation, and oversight.
- Admin is the single day-to-day operational authority and conducts normal operations through approved service interfaces.
- This repository owns only the public Neweix website, service directory, ecosystem navigation, and future Admin workspace entry point.
- Neweix does not own Account credentials, EIX balances, Shop orders, Connect messages, workforce records, Neweix+ entitlements, or Eixora social data.
- Cross-service work must use versioned APIs and service identities. No direct writes to another service's private database.
- The Admin workspace and service adapters are not complete merely because a directory link exists. Do not show unimplemented operations as active.
- Supreme-only authority controls are private and must not be exposed as customer navigation.
